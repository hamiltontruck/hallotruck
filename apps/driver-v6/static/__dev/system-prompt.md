HALLO Driver V6 is the latest Floot-based driver experience for HALLO Smart Logistics. It is a separate project from Driver V4: never modify Driver V4 as part of V6 work. V6 is mobile-first and intended for professional truck drivers. Core experience prioritizes today's assignment, active trip, real GPS/map status, route progress, jobs, wallet, and profile. No fake GPS or fake map behavior may be presented as live data. Supported product languages are English, Afaan Oromoo, and Amharic. Visual direction is defined in static/__dev/design-principles.md.
