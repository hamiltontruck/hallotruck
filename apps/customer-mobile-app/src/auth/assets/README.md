# Driver authentication artwork

These three raster assets were generated with the built-in image generator from the user's supplied `1000245929.jpg` design reference; they are recreations, not exact original source artwork.

- `splash.webp`: photorealistic white HALLO truck on a highway near Ethiopian hills at sunset, truck in the lower half and generous sky above, no UI text or device frame.
- `skyline.webp`: pale sage Ethiopian mountain, tower and gateway silhouette on the login canvas, wide footer illustration, no text.
- `wordmark.webp`: green HALLO lettering with a yellow road inside the A, transparent background, no tagline. Smart Logistics is rendered as accessible text beneath it.

WebP conversions keep the app assets compact. No phone bezel, notch or status-bar artwork is used in the app.

`google.svg` is the unmodified Google sign-in icon from https://www.gstatic.com/firebasejs/ui/2.0.0/images/auth/google.svg, vendored to avoid a third-party image request on the login page.
